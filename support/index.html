Quick help

Common questions

How do I LOCK?

Drag to rotate the world. When the two glowing path ends align, release. Timing closer to the center of the window earns a stronger grade.

Why is “Watch Ad” missing?

The optional rewarded ad is offered only on the first death of an Adventure run, and only when consent permits and an ad is ready. It is never required to keep playing.

My Prism balance looks wrong

Reconnect to the internet, reopen the game and allow the wallet to sync. Do not repurchase immediately. If it still looks wrong, contact us with the details below.

Can I restore a purchase?

The game is designed to recover purchased Prism through the server-side wallet using Apple-verified identity. StoreKit also retries unfinished purchases. After a reinstall on the same Apple Account, your remaining Prism, cosmetics and game progress (LF, missions, worlds, records) come back automatically once the game is online.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

When Google UMP requires it, open Help → Account & Privacy → Privacy Options in the game. Declining does not block the game; the ad option may simply be unavailable.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Help → Account & Privacy → Delete Account. If you no longer have the device, email the address below with “LOCKFRAME privacy request.” See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted, what is kept and what sensitive information never to send.

Real human support

Tell us what happened.

Email ogulcangogceloglu@gmail.com. If possible, include the app version, iOS version, approximate time of the problem and a screenshot. For purchase issues, include the product name and Apple transaction ID if visible—but never send your Apple ID password, Keychain secret, identity token or a full signed transaction.
